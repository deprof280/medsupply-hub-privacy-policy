MedSupply Hub Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect, and manage personal information

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MedSupply Hub (“MedSupply Hub,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you contact or use our WhatsApp chatbot and related services. It applies to information processed through the chatbot, including when we use the WhatsApp Business Platform or other service providers to operate it.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with MedSupply Hub and how the service is configured, we may process:

  • WhatsApp account and contact details: your phone number, WhatsApp display name, and identifiers supplied through WhatsApp.
  • Messages and enquiries: the messages, questions, replies, and information you choose to send to the chatbot, such as product enquiries, availability questions, or order-related requests.
  • Order or delivery details: where relevant and if you choose to provide them, details needed to respond to an order enquiry or arrange fulfilment.
  • Technical and service information: message timestamps, delivery status, error logs, and limited technical information needed to maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

Please do not send payment-card details, passwords, government identification numbers, or sensitive medical information through the chatbot unless we explicitly provide a suitable and secure process for that purpose.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • respond to questions about medical supplies and related products or services;
  • process and follow up on product, availability, or order enquiries;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and troubleshoot the chatbot;
  • detect abuse, spam, fraud, or other security issues;
  • meet applicable legal obligations and handle legitimate service-related disputes; and
  • improve the service using aggregated or otherwise appropriately de-identified information where feasible.

We will not use personal information for unrelated purposes in a way that is inconsistent with this policy or applicable law.

3. WhatsApp and Other Service Providers

MedSupply Hub operates through WhatsApp. Meta and WhatsApp may process information under their own terms and privacy policies when you use their services. We may also rely on hosting, chatbot, automation, customer-support, or AI service providers to operate MedSupply Hub. Where these providers process information for us, their access should be limited to what is needed to provide or secure the service.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where necessary to provide a service you request, protect the service or its users, comply with the law, or respond to lawful requests. Any additional sharing practices must be accurately described here before publication.

4. AI-Assisted Responses

The chatbot may use automated systems or AI tools to interpret enquiries and generate responses. Do not rely on chatbot responses as a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional. MedSupply Hub is intended to provide medical-supply information and assistance, not to diagnose conditions or prescribe treatment. We will update this section if the chatbot's actual capabilities or data-processing arrangements differ.

5. Health and Sensitive Information

Please avoid sharing medical records, diagnoses, prescriptions, or other sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a clearly explained service and an appropriate process is available. If such information is received, it should be handled only as permitted by applicable law and our actual service procedures.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including responding to enquiries, maintaining appropriate service records, addressing security issues, and meeting legal requirements. Actual retention periods depend on the information and the systems used. Information that is no longer needed should be securely deleted or de-identified, subject to any legal retention obligations.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the nature of the information we handle to help protect it from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No system or method of transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Processing

Some providers supporting WhatsApp, chatbot hosting, automation, or AI features may process information in countries other than your own. Where applicable, we will take steps required by relevant data-protection laws for such processing or transfers.
